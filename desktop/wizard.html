Where do your winnings go?

Your Bitcoin receive address — where a winning block pays out. Set your own. If you leave it blank, any block you win is mined straight to the developer's address, and a confirmed reward can't be moved afterward — it's permanent, not temporary. You can change this anytime, but it only applies to blocks won after the change.
